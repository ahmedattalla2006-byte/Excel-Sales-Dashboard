# Excel Sales Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes sales performance by city, state, region, product category, and salesperson, with slicers for quick filtering.

![Sales Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

## About the Project

This project turns raw sales data into a single dashboard that answers questions such as:

- Which cities, states, and regions generate the most revenue?
- Which product category sells best?
- How do salespeople compare?
- How much revenue is lost to returns and discounts?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Slicers (Category, Person, Region, Returned)
- KPI cards

## Dashboard Contents

**KPIs:** Total Sales, Returned, Net Sales, Discounts, COGS

**Charts:**
- Top 10 Cities in Sales
- Top 10 States in Sales
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Persons
- Sum of Quantity by Person

**Slicers:** Category, Person, Region, Returned

## Key Insights

### Overall performance
- Total sales reached **168.5K**, with **159.5K net sales** after returns.
- Returns total about **8,970** (roughly **5.3%** of sales).
- Discounts are negligible (**116**, about 0.07% of sales).
- COGS is **146K** (about **86.7%** of total sales), leaving a gross margin of roughly **13.3%**.

### Category
- **Technology** leads with **69.7K** (about 41% of sales), followed by Furniture (**53.1K**, about 32%) and Office Supplies (**45.6K**, about 27%).

### Geography
- **New York City** is the top city with **21.4K** (about 12.7% of total sales), roughly double Los Angeles.
- The top 10 cities generate about **48%** of total sales.
- **New York** and **California** are the top states, together about **33%** of total sales.
- **East** is the strongest region (**50.9K**), and **South** is the weakest (**34.5K**).

### Sales team
- **Anna Andreadi** sold the most units (**901**), followed by Chuck Magee (781), Kelly Williams (715), and Cassandra Brandow (**480**).

## Recommendations

- Focus marketing and inventory on **Technology** and the **East** region.
- Investigate the **South** region and the lower sales volume of Cassandra Brandow.
- Analyze the causes of returns, which account for about 5% of sales.
- Grow sales in cities outside the top 10 to reduce dependence on a few markets.
